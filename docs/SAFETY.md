# Safety Policy

1. Do not use for unauthorized access, surveillance, evasion, weapons, targeting,
   self-replication, or harm.
2. Do not collect personal, biometric, voice, image, EEG or HRV data without explicit
   informed consent and appropriate legal/ethical review.
3. Do not present symbolic or metaphysical metrics as scientific proof of consciousness.
4. Use sandboxing, tests, human approval and rollback for consequential changes.
5. Robotics integrations require a physical emergency stop and independent hard safety.
